# Agent-to-agent co-sell protocol

Partner agents work for another company. They share only what their policy allows. Be specific and minimal.

## Request format
Send one message per partner with:
- **Opportunity**: account name, region, industry, deal size band (for example "$250K-$500K"), target close month.
- **Why them**: the blocker you believe they remove, in one sentence.
- **Ask**: (1) do you have a relationship with this account, (2) can your team engage before the close date, (3) what are your standard co-sell terms.

Never send: customer contact details, exact contract values, your internal win probability, or other partners' names.

## Reading replies
- **Committed**: relationship confirmed, capacity available, terms stated.
- **Conditional**: interested but capacity or timing limits; note the condition.
- **Declined**: no relationship or no capacity before the close date.

## Output
A table of partners with status, relationship, earliest start, and terms, then a recommendation for the primary partner and a backup.
