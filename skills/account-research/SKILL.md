# Account research

## What to produce
A short brief with four parts:
1. **Situation**: what the customer is trying to do and by when.
2. **Signals**: 2 to 4 recent signals (funding, hiring, leadership change, contract renewals, tech changes). For each, say what it means for this deal.
3. **Decision path**: champion, economic buyer, and the gate that is slowing things (procurement, security, finance).
4. **Implication for partners**: which kind of partner would remove the blocker (cloud marketplace, integrator, software partner) and why.

## Rules
- Use only facts from your tools. Mark anything inferred as "likely".
- Prefer signals with a date. Older than 6 months is background, not a signal.
- Keep it under 150 words. The reader is another agent who needs facts, not prose.
