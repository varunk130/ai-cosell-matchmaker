# Partner fit scoring

Score each partner 0 to 100 with these weights:

| Factor | Weight | Full credit when |
|---|---|---|
| Solves the blocker | 32 | Removes the deal's main blocker (secondary blocker: 60%) |
| Already in the account | 20 | Active project (existing customer: 60%) |
| Cloud commit | 14 | Customer can pay from unused commit on that partner's cloud |
| Industry expertise | 14 | Partner specializes in the customer's industry |
| Co-sell track record | 12 | High historical co-sell win rate |
| Region coverage | 8 | Partner has a team in the customer's region |

## Recommend only if
- Score is 50 or more, and
- the partner solves at least one blocker, and
- the partner covers the region.

A cloud marketplace gets no blocker credit unless the customer buys on that cloud.

## Output
Top three partners with score, the two strongest reasons each, and any risk (capacity over 75%, slow to engage for a late-stage deal). Recommend which two to contact first.
