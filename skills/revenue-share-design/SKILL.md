# Revenue share design

## Structures
- **Marketplace private offer**: customer buys on their cloud bill. Marketplace fee is usually 3%. Counts against the customer's committed spend, which often removes procurement delays.
- **Referral fee**: partner introduces and supports; you pay 8 to 12% of first-year value.
- **Services-led**: partner sells implementation services alongside; no fee, but you depend on their delivery timeline.
- **Resale**: partner resells at a 15 to 20% margin; use only when the customer can only buy through that partner.

## How to compare
For each structure, compute: partner cost, net first-year revenue, win probability, and expected value (net revenue x win probability). Use historical win rates for "with high-fit partner" vs "no partner".

## Recommend
The structure with the highest expected value, unless it adds a dependency on a partner that is over capacity. State the terms in one line a seller could paste into an email.
