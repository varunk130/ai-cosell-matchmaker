# Account plan writing

## Co-sell plan structure (Markdown)
1. **Summary**: one paragraph. Deal, blocker, chosen partner, structure, expected impact.
2. **Why this partner**: three bullets, each tied to evidence.
3. **Partner responses**: short table of who was contacted and what they said.
4. **Commercial terms**: the recommended structure and the numbers.
5. **Risks and mitigations**: two or three bullets.
6. **Next steps**: three actions, each with an owner and a date.

## Intro email
Addressed to the partner manager by first name. Under 150 words. Lead with the opportunity, then why them, then one clear ask.

## Style
Plain language, short sentences, numbers over adjectives. No claims that are not in the findings you were given.
